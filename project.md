## Project description

This is to be a website that will be the front page for our organisation.
We are a state government department that supports the Australian grains industry through the development of digital tools and generation of genotype data with the Australian Grains Genebank as the centrepiece. 

We have developed an integrated digital tool ecosystem combined with a data standard that supports industry to make the most of AGG data and connect it to past, present and future pre-breeding projects

We have released 80,000 and counting genotypes publically, we are also the largest and are building a suite of open source tools. Our goal is to support the industry with open digital tools, bridging the gap between bioinformatics and breeding. We want the genes industry to make decisions based on data that is easy to access and use.
